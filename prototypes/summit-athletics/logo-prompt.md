# Summit Athletics — Logo Generation Prompt

## Context
Summit Athletics is a fitness gym chain with multiple locations. They offer personal training, group classes (Spin, HIIT, Yoga, Strength, Boxing), recovery zones, and nutrition coaching. Their tagline is "Your Strength. Our Community."

## Primary Color
Teal: `#0E8A7D` (oklch hue ~178)

## Prompt for AI Image Generation

> A professional corporate logo for "Summit Athletics" — a fitness gym chain. 
> The logo features a stylized mountain peak combined with a dumbbell or barbell silhouette, suggesting both summit/achievement and strength training. 
> The color palette is vibrant teal (#0E8A7D) with lighter teal accents. 
> The style is modern, energetic, and aspirational — conveying strength, community, and peak performance. 
> Clean vector-style illustration on a transparent background. 
> No text in the logo mark itself.

## Notes
- The logo should work at small sizes (32x32 favicon) and large (hero banner)
- Must have transparent background for overlay on light/dark themes
- Teal hue should match oklch(0.50 0.10 178)
- Fitness/athletic iconography: mountains, peaks, dumbbells, barbells, human figures in motion
- Should feel premium and modern, not aggressive
