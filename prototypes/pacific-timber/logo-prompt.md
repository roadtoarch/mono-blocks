# Pacific Timber Co — Logo Generation Prompt

## Context
Pacific Timber Co is a full-service forestry company in British Columbia. Operations span silviculture (seedling/nursery), logging, hauling, and lumber milling — a complete seed-to-finished-lumber vertical.

## Primary Color
Warm Brown: `#8B5E3C` (oklch hue ~55)

## Prompt for AI Image Generation

> A professional corporate logo for "Pacific Timber Co" — a forestry and lumber milling company. 
> The logo features a stylized beaver (the company mascot) or a combination of a tree trunk cross-section showing growth rings with sawmill blade elements. 
> The color palette is warm earthy brown (#8B5E3C) with amber accents (#A07040). 
> The style evokes craftsmanship, tradition, and sustainable forestry. 
> Clean vector-style illustration on a transparent background. 
> No text in the logo mark itself.

## Desired Mascot
A beaver — symbolizing industriousness, engineering, and the timber industry.

## Notes
- The logo should work at small sizes (32x32 favicon) and large (hero banner)
- Must have transparent background for overlay on light/dark themes
- Brown hue should match oklch(0.50 0.10 55)
- Forest/timber iconography: trees, growth rings, lumber, saw blades, beavers
- The beaver should look professional, not cartoonish
